Conceptual overview of SeisFrame automated reinforced-concrete frame design using a mutation-driven genetic algorithm. Building geometry, loading and material rules define a design case. Multiple candidate designs are evaluated, promising candidates are retained, and engineering-guided mutations propose changes before full re-evaluation. The visual mutation families summarize member strength, strong-column/weak-beam balance, joint and reinforcement compatibility, and drift control; random exploration is also retained in the search but is omitted from this overview for clarity. Geometry and concrete grade are design variables; reinforcement is selected and checked by the evaluator. Selection does not itself establish code acceptance or qualification. Required final checks and qualification must be completed before accepting and saving a design as qualified. Illustrations are schematic and do not present a validated design or measured performance improvement. The figure describes the workflow, not the current validation status of the software.
