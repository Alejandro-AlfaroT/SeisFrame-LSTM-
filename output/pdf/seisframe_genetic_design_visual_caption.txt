Figure X. Engineering-guided genetic search for automated RC frame design. The population is evaluated, checked and selected before mutation and repair generate new candidates. H1-H5 target strong-column/weak-beam behavior, member strength, bar fit, joint shear/anchorage and drift/stability; R1 supplies random exploration. Severe beam-flexure deficits can favor screened demand-sized jumps. Each new candidate still receives a full evaluation. Code-accepted designs enter the archive; independent qualification precedes qualified design output. Check bars show schematic normalized utilization relative to unity; the dashed row represents unresolved evidence. Illustrative frame and check icons are schematic, not analysis results.

Methods detail: the diagram condenses a mutation-driven controller with no crossover. Survivor selection occurs at generation boundaries; offspring are evaluated serially. Reinforcement is selected by the evaluator, while grouped dimensions and concrete grades form the chromosome. Unknown evidence is retained separately and cannot be treated as a passing check. Dashed output arrows denote separate downstream qualification. Research validation and engineering review gates remain in force; this schematic does not claim that they have been cleared.
