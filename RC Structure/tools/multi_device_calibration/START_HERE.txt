MOST-DAMAGED BEAM HINGE GRAPHS (AUTOMATIC)
After a device command finishes or stops, each usable trial in a completed search
gets a six-panel PNG: moment versus rotation, moment versus time, and rotation
versus time for BOTH local bending axes. The highest saved damage ratio among
BEAM ends selects the hinge; the graph labels its member, end, floor, pair and scale.
The calibration score still uses the structure-wide peak damage across all members.
The selected calibration trial is marked, and failed/unmet searches stay labeled.
CSV values and JSON provenance accompany every PNG. No new NTHA is needed.

Device graphs: outputs/wcalV3_r1_beam_plots/device_01_report (number matches device).
Keep the complete original device result folders; graphs do not replace them.
Collection regenerates graphs in outputs/wcalV3_r1_collection/peak_beam_hinge_plots.
To regenerate only a device's graphs from saved results:
    python -X utf8 -B pilots/v2WeightedCalibrationV3/school.py plots --device 1

Beam-hinge selection is per usable trial. It can change as intensity changes.
All-zero beam damage is labeled, and there is no column fallback. For the previous
all-member selection, use school.py plots --member-scope all with a fresh output.
Read peak_hinge_plots.json for skipped or invalid trials and any export errors.
The plots use scheduled committed snapshots, including the gravity response;
they do not represent every adaptive substep or a fitted fatigue-damage history.
